My apps

Formora

A smart body tracker

The scale isn't the whole story

I made it for women first. As a woman I know how much the body can change over a month, and how much it matters to take that into account — so as not to be upset by every new number on the scale.

You can track weight and measurements, keep progress photos and compare shots from different periods easily. And it helps you notice changes the scale does not always show: the weight barely moves while the volumes go down. Useful for men too — you can just leave the menstrual cycle feature off.

Download on theApp Store

Medistory

Everything you need to tell the doctor

And remember to ask

Have you ever got to the doctor and forgotten to mention some medication you are taking, or a chronic condition. Or forgotten to ask something important — then this app was made exactly for you.

With it you can fill in your profile beforehand with everything about your health, write down all the questions you need, and arrive prepared. The summary can be shown on your phone or printed. Everything stays on the phone.
